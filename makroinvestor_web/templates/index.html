What is Macro Investor?

A guide to what this site does, what it doesn't do, and how to read the numbers.

Purpose

This site translates macroeconomic data (PMI, inflation, employment, rates, energy, volatility) into a business-cycle phase (Early / Mid / Late / Recession) for Europe and the USA separately, and uses that phase to rank GICS sectors by which ones historically tend to perform best in that phase. It is a decision-support tool for thinking about sector rotation — not a price forecast, not investment advice, and not a guarantee of future returns.

How it works

  • Dashboard — current phase per region + sector ranking from the latest available macro data.
  • Multi-quarter — a heatmap of sector scores across historical and projected quarters.
  • My Expectations — a sandbox where you input your own macro assumptions and see how the phase and sector ranking change. Useful for scenario testing ("what if oil spikes 30%?").
  • Sectors — per-sector detail: which indicators drive its score, related ETFs and stocks.

Important limitations

The indicator weights, phase thresholds, and sector-sensitivity scores are expert judgement encoded as rules — they are not statistically fitted to historical returns. The model has not been formally backtested against realized sector performance. Treat its output as one structured opinion among several inputs to your own research, not as a signal to trade on directly. Live data sources (ECB/FRED) can fail; when they do, the site falls back to a manually-updated snapshot that may be stale by days or weeks.
